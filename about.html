A little project for a better keyboard.

An independent WebHID driver for the Everglide AE64 Pro.
Made to give every key a little more possibility.

  • Dial in actuation & Rapid Trigger
  • Remap keys, tune RGB, make it yours
  • Explore gamepad controls with custom firmware

Thanks for stopping by. Happy tinkering! :)
